## 3. Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Voley Score is the perfect digital scoreboard for your beach volleyball matches. Fast, clear and hassle-free: keep track of points and sets with a single tap, straight from your phone.

Built for players and referees, its interface is designed to be read at a glance, even in bright sunlight on the sand. Forget paper scoreboards and arguments over the score.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WHAT DOES VOLEY SCORE DO?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• Big, clear scoreboard
Each team's points on a full-screen display, with large buttons to add points instantly and without mistakes.

• Single match or best-of-3 sets
Choose the format you need. The app handles set changes and the deciding set automatically.

• Automatic court switch
Every 7 points (adjustable) the app notifies you and switches the teams' sides, also flipping the scoreboard so it always matches the real court.

• Customisable settings
Set the points per set (21 by default), the deciding set (15), the court-switch interval and the match type. Every match adapts to your rules.

• Your team colours
Assign each team its own background colour to tell them apart at a glance and avoid confusion.

• Match history
Every match is saved with date, time, result, per-set breakdown and duration. Check your results whenever you want.

• Clear end of match
When the match ends, the app shows the winner and the final score. Tap the board to bring the summary back whenever you need it.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
MADE FOR THE SAND
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• Landscape interface, ideal for placing it next to the net or the court.
• Large buttons to use with one hand.
• High contrast and big text for reading in the sun.
• Works offline: everything is stored on your device.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SIMPLE AND PRIVATE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Voley Score needs no sign-up or account. We do not collect your personal data: all your match information is stored only on your own phone.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PERFECT FOR
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• Beach volleyball and park volleyball players.
• Coaches and referees who want a reliable scoreboard.
• Tournaments and casual matches with friends.
• Anyone who wants a clear, distraction-free volleyball scoreboard.

Download Voley Score and focus only on playing. We'll take care of the scoreboard.
```
